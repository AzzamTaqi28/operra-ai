# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in the Operra project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the project, not the person.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or demeaning comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not
align with this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you need to report a problem, contact the project maintainers through the
repository's issue tracker or the documented maintainer contact method.
